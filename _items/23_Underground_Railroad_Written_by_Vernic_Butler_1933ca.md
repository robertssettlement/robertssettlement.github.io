"A typewritten manuscript of "'The Underground Railroad'" written by Vernice Butler and Published by John Carey in Noblesville, Indiana. The exact date of the manuscripts publishing is unknown, but it is though to be sometime in the 1950s.

"'The Underground Railroad'" is a history of the Underground Railroad in Indiana. It contains information on known routes through Indiana, methods of evading capture, and stories about altercations with slave catchers."
