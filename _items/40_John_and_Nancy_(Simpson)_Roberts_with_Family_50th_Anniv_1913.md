"A group photograph from John Roberts and Nancy Roberts' (née Simpson) 50th Wedding Anniversary. The anniversary was held at Roberts Settlement in 1913.

The individuals in the photo are identified from left to right.

Backmost Row (standing): Lester Roberts, Dr. Carl Robert, Lloyd Roberts, and Thaddeus Winburn.

2nd to back row (standing): Eliza (Simpson) Keene, Rhoda Matthews Roberts, Lucille Williams Roberts, Sarah -preffered name Sadie- (Bridgeman) Roberts, Alzadia (Roberts) Winburn, Clara (Roberts) Cooke, Clayton Cooke, Veora Simpson Mahoney (?), and Amy Evalina (Roberts) Sweat (?).

Middle Row (sitting in chairs): Nancy Elvira (Simpson) Roberts and John Anthony Roberts 

Second from the front (sitting): Estella Marie Roberts, Howard Cooke, Aleatha Winburn, Arvie Winburn, and Flora Edith Roberts.

Front row (sitting): Catherine Irene Roberts, Bernice Keene, Alva Winburn, and Esther Winburn."
