Lucetta and Alzadia were double cousins, both descendants of Keziah Corbin and Elijah Roberts. Martha Lucetta (left), preffered name Lucetta, was a direct descendant of Priscilla Hunt and Hansel Roberts, who was Elijah’s second cousin. They were active participants in the organization of early Roberts Settlement Homecomings.
