"Rachel was born May 1859 to Moody Gilliam and Julia Ann Walden in Marion Township, Boone County, Indiana. Her father, Moody Gilliam and his first wife Amanda Cannaday were thought to be the first colored settlers in Boone County.

Rachel's mother, Julia Ann, was the daugther of Micajah Walden an early settler at Roberts Settlement.

Rachel lived with her brother, Willlard and his wife, Lucetta (Roberts) Gilliam on their farm in the outskirts of Sheridan, Indiana (near Boxley) at the end of her life. She died June 5, 1920 from complications of Tuberculosis, and is buried at Roberts Settlement
"
