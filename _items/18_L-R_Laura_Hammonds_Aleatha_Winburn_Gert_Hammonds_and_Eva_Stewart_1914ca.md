"Photograph of four cousins taken circa 1914 in Hamilton County. From left to right they are: Laura Hammons, Aleatha Winburn, Gertrude, preferred name Gert, Hammonds and Eva Stewart.

Laura and Gert were sisters, daughters of Armesa Winburn and Thomas Hammons. Aleatha was the daughter of Alzadia Roberts and Thaddeus Winburn. Eva was the daughter of Mary Eliza Winburn and William Steward.  Aleatha and Eva were their their (Laura and Gert’s) first cousins "
