"The exact date of this photo is unknown, it is believed to be before 1910 and likely held at Brown’s Grove / Federal Hill in Noblesville, IN.

Reunions of descendants from Roberts Settlement occurred in different forms prior to the official Roberts Settlement Reunion which started in 1924. Stephen Roberts was the president and chief organizer.

The first organized Winburn Roberts Reunion occurred at Brown’s Grove/Federal Hill in Noblesville, Indiana in August 1899. Prior to that the families convene at various farms for what were known as “old settler meetings."""
