"A photograph of children standing in front of a house circa 1924.

Names are included on back of photo. From left to right: Elizabeth Dolby, Vernice Butler, Alice Butler, Kathryn Butler, and Jeanetta Duvall.

Jeanetta (Gilliam) Duvall,a descendant of Roberts Settlement, stated in an oral interview with Dr. Stephen Vincent that the Butler girls were her friends and palymates when she grew up, they lived a short distance away from the farm of Minnie (Rice) and Alonzo Gilliam, Jeanetta’s parents."
