Portrait of Martha Lucetta Gilliam circa 1892. In this photograph Martha is approximately 18 years old.

Martha was known as Lucetta.

She was born November 13, 1874 to Mary Eliza (Rice) and William Penn Roberts at Roberts Settlement. She married Willard Gilliam (son of Moody Gilliam and Julia Ann Walden of Boone County, Indiana) in 1894.
