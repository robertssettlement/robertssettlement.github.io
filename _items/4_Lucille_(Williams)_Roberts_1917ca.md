"Lucille was born October 25, 1886 in Vicksburg, Mississippi to George Williams and Josephine Bowens.

She married Dr. Carl Glennis Roberts on January 1, 1908 in St. Joseph County, Indiana. Lucille was a Chicago socialite. She and Carl had two children, their daughter Beverly and their son Carl Glennis Jr. Beverly was adopted.

Lucille died September 11, 1961 in Chicago, Illinois. She is buried beside Dr. Carl Roberts at Roberts Settlement in Atlanta, Indiana."
