"According to Master Thesis written by Edgar C. Conkling, “Roberts Settlement—A Mixed -Blood Agricultural Community in Indiana” the schoolhouse was built in 1877 and located at Roberts Corner, about a third of a mile east of Roberts Chapel on Settlement Road (now E. 276 Street).  
It sat next to the “Old” Cemetery (Hurley) on a small hill overlooking the creek. Although the school was patronized by primarily settlement families, there were a few white children in attendance because it was the most convenient school for them to attend. The teachers came primarily from Roberts Settlement.
As public schools in the area became consolidated, during the period between WW I and WW II, attendance declined and the school was eventually razed, although the exact date has yet to be determined.
Dr. Carl Roberts is sitting in the front row on the far right. 
Jesse Devaney, the Jackson Township Trustee is standing in back row on the far left. 
Former Roberts Settlement descendents that taught at Schoolhouse #5 include Lizzie Rice, Willard Gilliam, Marcus Gilliam, Maud Roberts, and Adorah Knight."
