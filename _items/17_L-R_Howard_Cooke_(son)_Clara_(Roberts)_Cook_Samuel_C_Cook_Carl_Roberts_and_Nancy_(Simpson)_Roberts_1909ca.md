"Family photograph likely taken at home of John and Nancy Roberts circa the 1900s.

The exact people in the photograph not certain. It is thought that subject of the photograph from left to right are: Howard Cooke, Clara Roberts Cooke, Clayton Cooke, Dr. Carl Roberts, and Nancy (Simpson) Roberts.


It could also be from left to right: Harnish Herschel Sweat holding a portrait of his sister, Loretta Loran Sweat (deceased), Amy Evalina (Roberts) Sweat,  Benjamin Sweat (husband of Amy and father of Harnish and Loretta). Dr. Carl Roberts and Nancy (Simpson) Roberts, are known to be the last two figures on the right."
