A typewritten history of the Roberts and Simpson families. It was prepared by L.L. Roberts and presented to John Roberts and Nancy (Simpson) Roberts at their 50th wedding anniversary.

Handwritten note on the back of the first page reads, "Prepared by L. L. Roberts Jun 24 1913 for Golden Wedding Anniversary of John A. and Nancy Roberts.
