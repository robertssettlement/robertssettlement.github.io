"A photograph of Lloyd Roberts and Sarah (Bridgeman) Roberts in front of his chiropodist office in South Bend, Indiana circa 1925. 

Lloyd was born at Roberts Settlement to John Roberts and Nancy (Simpson) Roberts on December 1, 1877. Sarah Bridgeman, preferred name Sadie, was born, November 12, 1867.

Lloyd and Sadie married November 8, 1905 in South Bend, Indiana. They ran two businesses from their home on South Taylor Street in South Bend. Lloyd was a chiropodist and Sadie was a beautician.

Sadie died March 21, 1927 and Lloyd died September 3, 1941.

Lloyd and Dr. Carl Roberts were brothers."
