A reprint of Dr. Carl Roberts' WGN Radio talk "A New Birth of Freedom," in the Fairmont News newspaper, printed November 17, 1938. 

In his radio address, Dr. Carl Roberts reflects upon Abraham Lincoln's Gettysburg address, and invokes the values of Christianity and Democracy to argue for the continued fight for racial equality.
