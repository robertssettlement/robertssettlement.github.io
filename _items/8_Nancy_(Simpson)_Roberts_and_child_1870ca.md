L"Nancy Elvira Simpson was born December 6, 1844 at Beech Settlement in Rush County, Indiana to Martha (Watkins) and Jacob Simpson.

Nancy's parents were free people of color from North Carolina and Virginia. They were a part of an out-migration in search of better opportunities from the tumultuous pre-Civil War South.

She married John Anthony Roberts on June 24, 1863 in Van Buren County, Michigan. John was the son of Keziah Corbin and John Roberts, the Roberts Settlement Founders. Nancy and John farmed on the land inherited from his parents at Roberts Settlement. They had ten children, six of which lived into adulthood.

Two of their sons became doctors, Carl (a surgeon) and Lloyd (a chiropodist) Two daughters were ministers or evangelists, Alzadia and Clara. Lester worked at a livery station in Kokomo, Indiana and  also worked also a janitor at the Hamilton County Courthouse in Noblesville. Amy Evalina was a practical nurse.

Nancy died May 2, 1917 in Noblesville, IN. She is buried at Roberts Settlement."
