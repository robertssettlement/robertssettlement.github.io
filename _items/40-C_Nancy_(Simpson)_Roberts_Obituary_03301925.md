"A typed obituary of Nancy (Simpson) Roberts. 

It identified Nancy Elvira (Simpson) Roberts as the daughter of Jacob and Martha Simpson. She was born in Rush County, Indiana on December 6, 1844 and died March 30, 1925.

Nancy Roberts married John Roberts June 24, 1863. They had ten children.

The obituary outlines Nancy's contributions to the Weslyan Methodist Church."
