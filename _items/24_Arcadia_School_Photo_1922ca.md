"A group photo of students in front of Arcadia School taken in Arcadia, Indiana circa 1922.

Some of the students lived at Roberts Settlement  attended Arcadia School after Schoolhouse No. 5 was closed.

Names included on back of photo:
""Nina Kennedy; Lewis Baldwin; Lloyd Clark; Johnny Cunningham; Leo Ridge; William Cunningham; Juanita Sweat; Louise Clark; Vernice Butler; Mabel Newson; Ray Langolf; Earl Baldwin; Cleo Ridge; Harrison Baker; Louis Cunningham; Ethel Ehman; Margaret Langolf; Inez Clark; Ray Clark; Bertha Baldwin; Louise Dickerson; Mildred Clark; Alice Butler; Grace Langolf;"""
